# Monmouth Dock & Dine — Dock Access Verification Audit

**Audit date:** September 12, 2026  
**Rule:** A restaurant is not presented as a usable Dock & Dine destination unless current evidence supports an actual customer/transient docking path. Waterfront location, “dockside” wording, photos of a pier, historic access, reviews, or an old Dock & Dine list are not enough by themselves.

## Status definitions

- **Verified customer dock** — current restaurant/marina/municipal source explicitly says dining guests can dock, use a Dock & Dine slip, or reserve a berth tied to dining.
- **Verified marina access** — current marina/municipal source offers transient slips and the restaurant is on-site or directly walkable. The boater must secure the marina berth separately.
- **Needs re-verification** — current primary source does not clearly establish a usable customer berth, even if secondary/historical sources claim dock access.
- **No verified customer dock** — current evidence does not establish guest dockage, or credible evidence specifically says Dock & Dine is not offered. These are removed from navigation pins.

## Operating rule going forward

1. Discovery can begin with a tourism list, boating blog, map, review, photo, or historical reference.
2. A place cannot become a green/blue navigation pin until a current restaurant, marina, harbor or municipal source establishes a usable customer/transient docking path.
3. If primary and secondary sources conflict, choose the conservative status and withhold the pin.
4. Marina adjacency is not restaurant dock access. If access is through a marina, the site must say so explicitly and tell the boater to secure a marina berth.
5. Published depth is separate from access verification. A verified dock with no reliable current MLW/MLLW depth must show “depth not published” rather than an estimated number.
6. Verification is not a guarantee of same-day space. Boaters must still follow the restaurant/marina’s reservation or call-ahead procedure.
7. Audit dates and evidence links are displayed on every listing so stale evidence can be identified and rechecked.
