Approach desk

What matters before you point the bow inland.

Sandy Hook / Two Rivers

12 ft federal project to Highlands → 9 ft Shrewsbury → 6 ft Navesink

Route 36 fixed bridge at Highlands is about 61 ft vertical clearance. Strong current near the bridge; use the marked channel. At the Navesink/Shrewsbury junction, a submerged stone jetty is a known hazard.

Shark River / Belmar

Inlet reported ~12 ft MLW · Belmar Marina ~7 ft MLW

Ocean Ave bascule is about 15 ft MHW; Route 71 bascule about 13 ft; railroad bridge about 8 ft; Route 35 fixed about 50 ft. Cross-current can be strongest on ebb near Ocean Ave.

Manasquan / Brielle

Busy marked inlet · tidal current roughly 1.8 kt

Wind opposing tide can make the inlet hazardous. Heavy recreational and commercial traffic; follow current marks and use local knowledge when conditions deteriorate.

Raritan Bay / Keyport

Matawan Creek destination · verify local creek depth

Keyport dock-and-dine sits well west of Sandy Hook. This is the outlier on the county map: verify creek channel, tide and the restaurant/marina’s current dock assignment before committing inland.

Rumson watchlist

Not counted as dependable current dock-and-dine stops.

Barnacle Bill’s · Rumson

Current 2026 statewide boating lists include it, while a detailed local guide previously said restaurant dock-and-dine was not permitted and the marina’s own materials emphasize seasonal slips. The site marks it call first rather than inventing certainty.

Roots Steakhouse · former Salt Creek site

Roots’ Rumson location is still officially listed as coming soon. Salt Creek historically had a small private T-dock and warned deeper-draft boats about low tide. It remains on the map as a future waterfront stop, not an open restaurant.

How the live water check works

  • Each destination is assigned to the nearest practical NOAA tide-prediction station: Sandy Hook/Highlands, Sea Bright, Oceanic/Navesink, Red Bank, Shark River or Manasquan.
  • Where a credible MLLW/MLW dock or marina depth is published, estimated water now = that baseline + current NOAA-predicted water level relative to MLLW.
  • The green/amber/red check compares that estimate with your draft + your selected under-keel clearance. It is not a hydrographic survey and does not model shoaling inside a slip.
  • If a dock depth has not been published or is too ambiguous, the site displays the local tide but deliberately refuses to manufacture a depth.
