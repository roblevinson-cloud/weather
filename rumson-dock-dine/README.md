# Rumson Dock & Dine

Live tide-aware guide to waterfront dining/dock access in Rumson, New Jersey.

## Scope
Version 1 is deliberately Rumson proper. Current research as of September 12, 2026 does not support ~13 active dock-and-dine restaurants inside the borough. It tracks Barnacle Bill's and the former Salt Creek site, now becoming Roots Steakhouse.

## Live water methodology
- Local astronomical tide: NOAA 8531753 (Oceanic, Navesink River).
- Optional weather/surge adjustment: NOAA 8531680 (Sandy Hook), observed minus predicted.
- Estimated depth = reported low-water dock/marina depth + estimated current tide.

Planning aid only; not navigation-grade. Verify current dock policy, soundings, charts and under-keel clearance before arrival.

Sources: NOAA CO-OPS, Barnacle Bill's official marina page, Roots official site, Dining Out Jersey Dock & Dine guide, and historic Boater's Digest Dock-n-Dine guide.
