How to read the depth estimate

Estimated dock depth at arrival = published low-water dock baseline + NOAA predicted tide relative to MLLW. Where a separate approach baseline exists, the site also computes approach depth and treats the shallower of the two as controlling. If the restaurant or marina does not publish a defensible low-water baseline, the tide curve is shown but no dock depth is manufactured.

Important: predicted astronomical tide is not observed water level. Wind, barometric pressure, river flow, dredging and shoaling can materially change actual depth. Confirm current conditions with the dockmaster and use current NOAA ENC / Coast Pilot / Local Notice to Mariners plus the vessel’s depth sounder.
