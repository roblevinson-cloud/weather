How the live depth estimate works

  • Local astronomical tide: NOAA station 8531753, Oceanic / Navesink River in Rumson.
  • Weather / surge adjustment: observed minus predicted water level at NOAA Sandy Hook 8531680, added to the local Rumson prediction when available.
  • Dock depth estimate: reported depth at mean lower low water (MLLW) + estimated current local water level.
  • Go / no-go check: compares the conservative end of the depth range against your boat draft + selected safety margin.

Navigation warning: This is a planning aid, not a substitute for NOAA charts, current marina soundings, Notices to Mariners, local knowledge, or your depth sounder. Reported marina depths can be stale and conditions can differ by berth and approach channel. Always verify dock availability and depth directly before arrival. *Barnacle Bill’s MLW range comes from a third-party marina directory and should be treated as provisional until the marina provides a current sounding.
