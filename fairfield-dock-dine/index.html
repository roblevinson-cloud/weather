Approach desk

Waterway constraints that matter before the restaurant.

Greenwich / Stamford

Deep-water marina choices · strong tidal range

Greenwich Harbor and Stamford Harbor have substantial marina infrastructure. Harbor Point Dock & Dine assignments are made in real time; hail VHF 9 rather than choosing a slip yourself.

Norwalk / Five Mile River

Use the marked channels · shoal edges matter

Rowayton and Norwalk have several genuine boat-up restaurants, but local branches and mooring fields can be much shallower than the main harbor project channel.

Saugatuck / Westport

Shallow, narrow and tide-sensitive

The Saugatuck is the county's most tide-sensitive restaurant run in this guide. NOAA Coast Pilot warns of severe shoaling and recommends local knowledge and favorable tide.

Bridgeport / Stratford

Deep marina basins · Housatonic current

Bridgeport offers some of the strongest published dock-depth data. Farther east, the Housatonic can carry strong current at marina slips even when depth is ample.

How the tide and depth engine works

  • The site requests NOAA 6-minute predictions when the assigned station supports them. If NOAA rejects that interval because the station is subordinate, it automatically requests official high/low predictions and interpolates between them for planning display.
  • Estimated dock depth = published low-water dock baseline + predicted tide relative to MLLW. Approach depth is calculated separately when a published approach baseline exists.
  • Controlling estimate is the shallower of the available dock and approach estimates. It is compared with your boat draft plus selected under-keel clearance.
  • If the dock baseline is unknown, the card says so. A channel or approach depth is never relabeled as the restaurant's dock depth.
  • Source confidence describes the baseline, not NOAA's astronomical prediction. Directory soundings are useful planning inputs but are not hydrographic surveys; call the dockmaster for current conditions.
